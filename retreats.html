The Journey

Transformative Outcomes of the Retreat

What opens when you give yourself time and space in a held, trauma-informed container:

  • Nervous System Reset: Release chronic fight-or-flight tension through daily breathwork, elemental nature, and somatic grounding.
  • Emotional De-Armouring: Gently shed protective layers and unexpressed emotion in a space of unconditional acceptance and confidentiality.
  • Reclaiming Vital Sensuality: Reawaken the natural flow of life-force energy (Kundalini), pleasure, and embodied confidence without shame or pressure.
  • Authentic Belonging: Experience deep relational healing, vulnerability, and heart-felt connection with fellow participants.

Moments from Past Retreats

Our retreats take place in secluded natural sanctuaries in the sun-drenched hills of the Algarve, Portugal. Within the sacred space of traditional yurts, sunlit courtyards, and ancient olive groves, participants experience laughter, tears of liberation, tender connection, and joyous celebration. These glimpses from past gatherings honor the brave souls who chose to open their hearts and remember their wholeness.
